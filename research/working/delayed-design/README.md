# Certifying delayed multiplicative updates for linear experimental design

[Read the working paper and its full supplement](https://taitai2025.github.io/assets/documents/research/delayed-design.pdf)

The paper studies two specific cached-sensitivity rules. Their steps may improve strictly while infinitely many exact refreshes still leave a suboptimal limit. The refresh-count cap restores convergence of the design value. A fixed two-point instance measures the extra update cost for one capped rule. The four-type example uses large finite row multiplicities and is analytic. The original numerical comparisons count each computational resource separately.

Files in paper/ build with pdfLaTeX and the standard packages listed in the preamble. DesignFinite.lean checks 10 finite rational declarations in Lean 4.8.0. The epoch arguments and global convergence are proved in the paper. The companion exact-checks.json records 15 independently checked symbolic expressions and inequalities.

This working paper was prepared with AI assistance and checked by separate AI reviews. Human author verification is pending.
